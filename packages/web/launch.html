Mainnet routes

your approval required

Both routes keep the private balance inside Ready X while Facet supplies the app-specific instructions. Your wallet shows the final action before anything is submitted. The wallet binding preview above does not silently move money or approve a transaction.

Honest boundary

No silent signing

Connecting and signing the message above does not move money. The two live routes open separate review pages and ask for a transaction only after you choose the route and approve it in your wallet. The evidence page remains the place to verify completed receipts.
