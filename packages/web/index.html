How Facet protects you

Full detail

Fixed amounts, by policy

The intended policy uses shared funding steps, never a figure you type. An arbitrary amount like 137.42 STRK identifies you across every identity that uses it. The current launcher does not yet enforce the denomination policy.

Timing is visible

Funding and acting should be separated because two identities made seconds apart can correlate. Timing separation is a product policy, not a current guarantee of this page.

Self-linking calls are guarded

The tested Vesu and Endur builders refuse a call that pays your wallet, an address that funded your pool, or another identity. The browser path must use those builders before this is a launcher-wide guarantee.

Proving is asynchronous

Private actions still take minutes to prove on the current host. The target launcher queues a job, shows its stages, and lets you leave the page instead of hiding the wait.

What nothing can hide

Inherent, not a to-do list

The identity exists in public

An account has to be on chain to act. Anyone can see it and what it did. What stays hidden is whose it is, and that is the whole claim.

Six minutes is the real cost

Proving cannot be skipped. A warm worker and resumable job id can make the wait easier to live with, but they do not make the cryptographic work disappear.
