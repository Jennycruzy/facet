How Facet protects you

Full detail

Fixed amounts, not yours

Identities are funded only in set steps, never a figure you type. An arbitrary amount like 137.42 STRK identifies you across every identity that uses it. A step everyone else uses does not.

Separated in time

Funding and acting are spaced apart, so two identities made in one sitting do not appear on chain seconds apart wearing the same amount.

Self-linking calls are refused

Facet will not build an action that pays your wallet, an address that funded your pool, or another of your identities. It refuses rather than warns, because a warning gets clicked through.

Proving starts before you ask

The six minutes begin when you pick an app, not when you confirm. Most of the wait is spent while you are still deciding.

What nothing can hide

Inherent, not a to-do list

The identity exists in public

An account has to be on chain to act. Anyone can see it and what it did. What stays hidden is whose it is, and that is the whole claim.

Six minutes is the real cost

Proving cannot be skipped. It can be started early and shown honestly, and that is what Facet does. It cannot be made to disappear.
