What this does not hide

  • Amounts and timing are public. The funding leg names the token and the exact amount. Two faces funded with the same amount seconds apart are correlated in practice, whatever the cryptography says.
  • The face itself is public. What is hidden is whose it is, not that it exists.
  • A careless call can undo it. If the action itself names you, the receipt links the face to you. Our first rehearsal did exactly that, and it is written up on the proof page rather than quietly fixed.
  • Every action takes about six minutes to prove. That is the honest cost of the privacy, and the interface shows the real stages rather than a spinner.
